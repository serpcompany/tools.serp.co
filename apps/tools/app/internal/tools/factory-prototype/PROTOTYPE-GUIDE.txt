TOOL FACTORY TABLE — FIVE-MINUTE REVIEW

Start it
  pnpm dev:local

Open it
  http://localhost:3000/internal/tools/factory-prototype

This route is throwaway, local-only, and read-only. It is deliberately unavailable in production.

Minute 1 — Scan
  Scan the default Tool, status, operation, family, library, runtime, verification, and attention columns. Ask: can you understand the portfolio without opening anything?

Minute 2 — Find
  Search for a Tool, library, format, or blocker. Filter by status, family, runtime, and verification.

Minute 3 — Configure
  Open Columns. Show or hide anything. Sort headers, resize columns, and change rows per page.

Minute 4 — Inspect
  Click a Tool row. Check whether the full detail is understandable and whether an important fact is absent.

Minute 5 — Decide
  Name the columns you want visible by default, columns you never need, and any status/filter wording that is confusing.
